Privacy Policy

Focus Puppy – Study Timer
Last updated: October 20, 2026

Summary

Focus Puppy is designed to be private. Your focus history, coins and puppies stay on your device, and no account is needed. The only data that leaves your device is what Apple and RevenueCat need for purchases, and what Google AdMob needs when you choose to watch an optional bonus video (see “Advertising”).

Information we do NOT collect

  • No account or sign-up is required
  • No personal information (name, email, contacts, photos, precise location)
  • Your focus sessions, coins, decorations and settings are never sent to us or to advertisers
  • No access to your camera, microphone, or notifications

Information stored on your device only

Your focus history, coins, decorations, language preference and settings are stored locally on your device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple (App Store). We never see your payment details. To validate purchases and restore them across your devices, we use RevenueCat, which processes an anonymous device identifier and purchase history on our behalf. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Advertising (optional bonus videos)

Focus Puppy has no banner ads, no pop-up ads, and never shows ads during a focus session. The only ads are optional reward videos: in the Shop (and, if you like, after a completed session) you can choose to watch a short video to get bonus coins, up to 3 times a day. Premium members get the same bonus without any video.

These videos are provided by Google AdMob. When a video is requested, Google may collect and process information such as your IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions, and crash and performance diagnostics, in order to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud.

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): used only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. Focus Puppy asks at most once, after your first completed session — never on first launch. If you don't allow it, you get non-personalized ads and the app works exactly the same. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent: where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown first, and an “Ad privacy choices” option appears in the app's Settings so you can change your choice.
  • Content rating: ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (G).
  • Watching videos is always optional; skipping them never affects your progress.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children's privacy

Focus Puppy is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; bonus-video ads are limited to G-rated content.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
